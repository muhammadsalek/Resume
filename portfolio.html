Research Profile

I am a third-year undergraduate student of Statistics at Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST), Bangladesh, and a Research Fellow at CHINTA Research Bangladesh. My work lies at the intersection of epidemiology, spatial statistics, and machine learning — with a focus on maternal and child health disparities in South Asia and Sub-Saharan Africa.

I have authored or co-authored 15+ manuscripts currently under review in top-tier Q1 journals (Scientific Reports, BMC Women's Health, BMJ Open, PLOS ONE, Social Science & Medicine), and have one published article in BMC Women's Health (Springer Nature).

My methodological expertise spans complex survey analysis, machine learning with imbalanced data (SMOTE), geospatial/choropleth mapping, structural equation modelling, multilevel models, and interpretable AI (SHAP/LIME). I build end-to-end research pipelines from national DHS datasets to peer-reviewed publications and interactive Shiny applications.

I am seeking a funded PhD position in Biostatistics, Epidemiology, or Public Health to extend this work into causal inference, Bayesian spatial modelling, and global health inequalities.
